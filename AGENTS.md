# Agent instructions for this repository

This repository is a reusable skill called **Cold Read**: a method for reading a company from the public record alone, before investing in it, joining it, buying it, competing with it or partnering with it.

When a task asks you to research, understand, assess, size up or "read" a company, its acquisition, its engineering stack, its job ads, a named competitor, a prospective partner or supplier, or the people someone will meet there, read `skills/cold-read/SKILL.md` first and follow it. The lane briefs are in `skills/cold-read/references/lanes.md`, the sources and endpoints in `skills/cold-read/references/sources.md`, and the output templates in `skills/cold-read/references/outputs.md`. Sample reads are in `skills/cold-read/examples/`.

Non-negotiable rules from the skill: public record only; mark every claim ✅ cited, 🟡 inferred or ❔ unknown; never open a person's LinkedIn profile; cite DNS and certificate findings with the command that produced them and confirm them in words (ads, pages, filings) before quoting them to the company; keep any inside knowledge (including anything received under an NDA) in a separate, clearly marked section; never sign up for a trial, request a demo or use a pretext to read a competitor; read a note as its subject would before sharing it; date everything.
