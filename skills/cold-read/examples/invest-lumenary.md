# Lumenary Learning: price against what was bought, and whether it holds (3 February 2026)

Illustrative: all names, numbers and sources are invented. Legend: ✅ cited; 🟡 inferred; ❔ unknown.

## The answer in one paragraph

Lumenary is a listed school-software company whose growth since 2023 is two acquisitions, not its own product. The filings show it paid 2.1x revenue for a profitable assessment business and 9x for a loss-making tutoring marketplace, and the job boards show it is building on the second and running down the first. The three-year case rests on whether the tutoring marketplace reaches the margin the CEO has guided to; the signals to watch are two earnings calls and one earn-out cliff.
