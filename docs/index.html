Cold Read

Read any company from the outside before you invest, join or buy it. A skill for AI coding agents that works from the public record alone and marks every claim.

Get the skill on GitHub See example views

How it works

A company cannot hide its plan. It is in what it files, what it hires for, what it ships, what it tells investors, and what happened to companies like it. Cold Read reads six lanes, in parallel when the agent has sub-agents, and writes one note in the order a busy reader needs.

1Corporate record

Filings, registries, deals and their structure, purchase price allocation, directors, cash and debt.

2Counterparty or product

The other side of a deal in the same depth, or products, pricing, customers and money.

3Says versus does

Dated investor statements against releases, privacy pages, app stores and migrations.

4Market and base rate

The analyst split, the regulators, and five to eight comparables with what happened and when.

5Infrastructure fingerprint

DNS, certificate logs, headers, archived pages. Passive and read-only. Context, never talking points.

6Openings as the plan

The job boards as next year's org chart, pulled from the applicant-tracking APIs, not the careers page.

Plus a people lane for interviews, with no LinkedIn profile ever opened. Every claim is marked ✅ cited, 🟡 inferred or ❔ unknown.

Four uses

Invest

What did they pay for, and is it worth it? Price against what was bought, the earn-out as a signal, the dated calls that settle it.

Join

What would I inherit, and who will I meet? Stack by chapter, the mandate, questions by round, one-line reads of each person.

Deal

What happens to the acquired company, and was it cheap? Two deals side by side, the base rate, the cutover window, the surviving roles.

Position

Who wins the market, and what would change it? The moat as the filings show it, the regulator as the real constraint, three dated events.

Install

The skill is a folder with a SKILL.md, the Agent Skills format that Claude Code, Codex and other agents read. Rule files are included for Cursor and GitHub Copilot.

git clone https://github.com/atlas-apex/cold-read
cd cold-read
./install.sh claude     # ~/.claude/skills/cold-read
./install.sh codex      # ~/.codex/skills/cold-read
./install.sh all
./install.sh /path/to/your/agent/skills

Then ask your agent, in your own words:

  • "Cold read Acme Ltd before my interview on Thursday."
  • "Was Acme bought cheap, and what happens to its engineers?"
  • "Read Acme's job ads: what are they building next year?"
  • "Is Acme a three-year hold? Outside view only."

Cursor: copy .cursor/rules/cold-read.mdc into your project. Copilot: copy .github/copilot-instructions.md. Other agents: add AGENTS.md to their instructions.

Rules the skill enforces

Public record only

Filings, registries, press, job ads, public pages and APIs, DNS, certificate logs, archives. Nothing behind a login, no pretexts.

Every claim marked

✅ cited and linked, 🟡 inferred or from an unopened snippet, ❔ unknown after looking. Readers can tell research from gossip at a glance.

No profile views

LinkedIn profile visits tell the person they are being researched. Snippets, team pages, talks and filings instead.

Context, not talking points

Infrastructure findings shape the view and are never quoted to the company. Confirm from ads, press or filings before citing.

Inside knowledge stays separate

If you work there, your facts sit in a marked section. The shareable copy reconstructs from the sources alone.

Dates on everything

When each board was pulled and each page read. A reading is only as good as its date.
