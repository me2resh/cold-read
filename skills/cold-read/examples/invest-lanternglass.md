Sample read: the company is a sample and the figures are illustrative; the method, the sources and the marks are as shipped. Legend: ✅ cited; 🟡 inferred; ❔ unknown.

## The answer in one paragraph

Lanternglass is a listed school-software company whose growth since 2023 is two acquisitions, not its own product. The filings show it paid 2.1x revenue for a profitable assessment business and 9x for a loss-making tutoring marketplace, and the job boards show it is building on the second and running down the first. The three-year case rests on whether the tutoring marketplace reaches the margin the CEO has guided to; the signals to watch are two earnings calls and one earn-out cliff.
