# Meridian Fleet Software and Parcelwise: the integration, from the outside

Read on 14 March 2026. Public record only. Illustrative: all names, numbers and sources are invented. Legend: ✅ cited; 🟡 inferred; ❔ unknown.

## 1. The answer in one paragraph

Meridian bought Parcelwise for its 1,900 UK courier customers and its Royal Mail and DPD integrations, not for its platform. The purchase price allocation puts the integrations at twice the value of the technology, the Parcelwise brand at under a tenth of the price, and the two companies' job boards now describe one engineering organisation under Meridian's Leeds VP. The base rate for this kind of deal says the Parcelwise platform is absorbed 12 to 24 months after close, so between June 2026 and June 2027, with the customer-facing app the last thing to move 🟡.

## 7. Outside view and the base rate (excerpt)

Five comparables in UK vertical SaaS consolidation, 2019 to 2025: brand retired in four of five within 18 months; platform absorbed in all five within 24 months of close; the acquired CTO left within 12 months in four of five; customer-facing migration last ✅ [sources 14 to 19]. Analyst split: two Buys on cross-sell, one Hold on integration cost 🟡.

## 8. What it means for Parcelwise engineering 🟡

The carrier integrations and the Bristol support team are the assets Meridian paid for and will keep. The Parcelwise platform is the asset it did not pay for. Engineers on the integrations have 18 to 24 months of certain work; engineers on the web app and the mobile app have 9 to 15. The surviving roles are the ones that know the carrier APIs.
