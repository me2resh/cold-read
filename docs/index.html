A company cannot hide its plan

It is in what it files, what it hires for, what it ships, what it tells investors, and what happened to companies like it. Cold Read reads those six places at once and writes one note: the answer first, then the evidence, then what it means for you, then the dates that would prove it wrong.

  • Corporate recordWho owns it, what it bought or sold and for how much, who runs it, how much cash and debt.Securities filings, company registries, deal releases, director changes.
  • Counterparty or productThe other side of a deal in the same depth, or products, pricing, customers and money.Filings for the other party, pricing pages, app stores, customer counts.
  • Says versus doesWhat leadership tells investors against what the releases, privacy pages and migrations show.Earnings calls, shareholder letters, privacy and terms pages, archived pages, app listings.
  • Market and base rateHow outsiders rate it, the regulatory climate, and what happened in five to eight comparable situations.Analyst notes, trade press, regulator enforcement, named comparables.
  • Infrastructure fingerprintWhich platform serves which brand and how the estates are joined. Passive, read-only, and cited with the command that produced each finding.DNS, certificate transparency, response headers, archived pages, app metadata.
  • Openings as the planWhat the company is building, where, at what seniority, and what it has stopped hiring for.Applicant-tracking APIs rather than the careers page; aggregator snippets for closed ads.
  • PeopleWho you will meet, what they have said and written, and a one-line read of what each will test. Runs for interviews only.Team pages, talks, podcasts, books, blogs, repositories, filings. No LinkedIn profile is ever opened.

Four questions it is built for

Invest

What did they pay for, and is it worth it?

Price against what was bought, the earn-out as a signal, the dated calls that settle it.

Join

What would I inherit, and who will I meet?

Stack by chapter, the mandate, questions by round, one-line reads of each person.

Deal

What happens to the acquired company, and was it cheap?

Two deals side by side, the base rate, the cutover window, the surviving roles.

Position

Who wins the market, and what would change it?

The moat as the filings show it, the regulator as the real constraint, three dated events.

Install it in your agent

Pick your agent. Two minutes, no account.

One line, as a plugin. The skill then answers to /cold-read.

/plugin install cold-read --marketplace me2resh/cold-read

Older versions: /plugin marketplace add me2resh/cold-read, then /plugin install cold-read@cold-read. From a shell: claude plugin install cold-read --marketplace me2resh/cold-read. Update later with /plugin update cold-read@cold-read.

Codex reads skills from ~/.codex/skills. The installer copies the folder there.

git clone https://github.com/me2resh/cold-read
cd cold-read
./install.sh codex

Inside a project, AGENTS.md in the repository is also written for Codex: copy its text into your project's AGENTS.md and keep skills/cold-read/ where Codex can read it.

Cursor uses project rules. Copy the rule file into your project; it loads the skill when a task matches.

git clone https://github.com/me2resh/cold-read
mkdir -p .cursor/rules
cp cold-read/.cursor/rules/cold-read.mdc .cursor/rules/
cp -R cold-read/skills/cold-read .cursor/

The rule points at skills/cold-read/SKILL.md; keep the copied folder next to it or adjust the path in the rule.

Copilot reads repository instructions. Copy the instructions file and the skill folder into your project.

git clone https://github.com/me2resh/cold-read
mkdir -p .github
cp cold-read/.github/copilot-instructions.md .github/
cp -R cold-read/skills/cold-read .github/

If your project already has copilot-instructions.md, append the text instead of replacing it.

Any agent that reads a folder with a SKILL.md, or a plain instructions file, can use it.

git clone https://github.com/me2resh/cold-read
cd cold-read
./install.sh /path/to/your/agent/skills

For agents with a single instructions file, paste AGENTS.md into it and keep skills/cold-read/ readable by the agent.

Then ask the way you would ask a colleague.

  • Cold read Acme Ltd before my interview on Thursday.
  • Was Acme bought cheap, and what happens to its engineers?
  • Read Acme's job ads. What are they building next year?
  • Is Acme a three-year hold? Outside view only.

Six things it will not do

  • Go behind a login.Filings, registries, press, job ads, public pages and APIs, DNS, certificate logs, archives. No scraping against terms, no pretexts.
  • State a claim without a mark.Cited and linked, inferred from the record, or unknown after looking. Readers tell research from gossip at a glance.
  • Open a LinkedIn profile.A profile visit tells the person they are being researched. Snippets, team pages, talks and filings instead.
  • Quote a hostname before confirming it.DNS and certificate findings are public and go in the note with the command. They show that something exists, not why, so the skill confirms each one from an ad, a page or a filing before it is said to the company.
  • Mix inside knowledge with the record.If you work there, your facts sit in a marked section. The shareable copy reconstructs from the sources alone.
  • Leave a reading undated.When each board was pulled and each page read. A reading is only as good as its date.
