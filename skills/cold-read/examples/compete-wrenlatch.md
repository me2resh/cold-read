## 1. The answer in one paragraph

**The short answer**

- The opening in 2027 is the clinic of 2 to 6 practitioners that needs a staff rota and room allocation: Wrenlatch raised that clinic's price by 18 to 21 per cent in 2026 and shows no rota or rooms feature 🟡, because the rise is in the archived pricing pages but the missing feature is read from the documentation and the ideas board, not from a statement that it will never be built [^s3-5][^s3-6][^s2-4][^s2-14].
- When a small rival takes those clinics, Wrenlatch is likely to answer with a temporary cheap tier and a comparison page, not a product change 🟡, because that is what it did within 97 days of Ebbfleck's launch in 2024 [^s4-6][^s3-2][^s3-8].

**The evidence**

- The pricing page moved on 3 March 2026: Starter from GBP 24 to GBP 29, Practice from GBP 38 to GBP 45 and Group from GBP 52 to GBP 56 per practitioner per month, so the smallest clinics took the largest rise ✅ [^s3-5][^s3-6].
- 8 of its 11 open roles name clinic groups, multi-site or Ireland, and none names small clinics or support ✅ [^s6-1].
- 4 of the 7 releases in the last twelve months are gated to the top two tiers, and card payments are on those two tiers only ✅ [^s2-5][^s2-3].
- Against Ebbfleck it introduced a Solo tier at GBP 15 a month on 10 June 2024, wrote in the same post that Solo was "here to stay", and closed it to new sign-ups on 1 June 2025 ✅ [^s3-7][^s2-12].
- Customer count, churn and the revenue split by tier are not public ❔ not in the FY2025 accounts, the Series A release or the customers page [^s1-1][^s1-4][^s2-9].

**The position**

- Wrenlatch is moving up-market to clinic groups while charging small clinics more 🟡, a reading that rests on the price captures, the board and the changelog and on nothing the company has said [^s3-6][^s6-1][^s2-5].
- It keeps the advantage wherever a clinic wants card payments, reminders and a free import in one product, and card payments is the first row of its comparison page against Ebbfleck 🟡, because one page against one rival is a single precedent [^s2-13][^s2-8][^s3-8].

## 6. Where they are strong and where they are exposed

**Strong**

- Card payments are in the product on Practice and Group, with deposits and no-show fees, at 1.9 per cent plus 20p a transaction ✅ [^s2-13][^s2-3].
- Reminders go by email and SMS on every tier and have been two-way since 10 February 2026 ✅ [^s2-5][^s2-3].
- Reviewers praise the reminders: a review dated March 2026 says "no-shows fell as soon as patients could confirm by text" 🟡, from a snippet that could not be opened [^s4-2].
- Reach: 1,140 app ratings, a listing in a physiotherapy trade body's supplier directory since 2023 and two clinics that name it on their own booking pages ✅ [^s2-6][^s4-8][^s2-10][^s2-11].
- Switching in is cheap: the import tool is free and reads exports from nine other systems ✅ [^s2-8].
- Money: GBP 5.1m of cash at 31 December 2025, after a GBP 6.5m Series A ✅ [^s1-1][^s1-4].

**Exposed**

- The clinic of 2 to 6 practitioners pays 18 to 21 per cent more than in January 2026 🟡, because the rise is cited but which tier such a clinic buys is the pricing page's suggestion and not a count [^s3-5][^s3-6][^s2-1].
- A review dated April 2026 reads "a fifth more for the same diary, and nothing new for a clinic our size" 🟡, one person's account from a snippet that could not be opened [^s4-4].
- That clinic gets no staff rota and no room allocation, and the request for both is marked Not planned 🟡, because the board status and the documentation show the product on 12 November 2026 and not a decision that cannot change [^s2-14][^s2-4].
- Support is slowest on the tier where the price rose most 🟡, resting on the two-day reply target on Starter, the June 2026 review and a board with no support role [^s2-7][^s4-3][^s6-1].
- Payments is the least reliable component on its own status page, with incidents on 17 February, 4 June, 22 September and 13 October 2026 🟡, because a status page holds what the company chose to post and its history starts in January 2026 [^s5-1]; only the 22 September incident is confirmed in words elsewhere, by the post-incident note ✅ [^s3-12].
- Attention has moved: 8 of 11 roles and 4 of 7 releases point at groups 🟡, a reading of the board and the changelog, and a company can serve two segments at once [^s6-1][^s2-5].
- Its word on low prices is weak with small clinics: the tier it called "here to stay" in June 2024 closed in June 2025 🟡, because the two dates are cited and how clinics read them is not in the record [^s3-7][^s2-12].

## 8. How they are likely to respond

Ebbfleck is the one earlier entrant in the record: it launched on 16 April 2024 with a plan for sole practitioners at GBP 15 a month ✅ [^s4-6]. Every row below is read from what Wrenlatch did next.

| If you | They are likely to | Rests on |
|---|---|---|
| Take clinics of 2 to 6 practitioners from them on price, rota and rooms | Bring back a low-priced tier for small clinics for a limited period, about two months after they notice 🟡 | Ebbfleck launched 2024-04-16; the Solo tier followed on 2024-06-10, 55 days later, and closed on 2025-06-01 [^s4-6][^s3-7][^s2-12] |
| Say in public that you have rota and rooms and they do not | Publish a comparison page that names you and leads with card payments, and leave the product as it is 🟡 | The page against Ebbfleck is dated 2024-07-22, 97 days after the launch; the rota request was set to Not planned on 2026-01-15 [^s3-8][^s2-14] |
| Win clinics that already use them | Add your export format to the free import tool, to shorten the way back 🟡 | The import tool shipped on 2024-09-03, 140 days after the launch, and read Ebbfleck exports from its first version [^s2-8] |
| Add card payments | Move on card fees before they move on features 🟡 | No precedent in the record: Ebbfleck's pricing page shows no card payments, so the case has not arisen [^s4-7] |
| Sell to multi-site groups or open in Ireland | Answer with sales effort and not with price, since that is where the open roles are 🟡 | No precedent in the record; the reading rests on the board of 2026-11-12 [^s6-1] |

**What the precedent does and does not show**

- In 2024 the response was price and marketing: the changelog has no feature built for one-practitioner practices beyond the tier itself ❔ not in the 19 entries dated 2024 [^s2-5].
- The cheap tier closed 12 days after the Series A release of 20 May 2025 🟡, because the two dates are cited and the link between them is a reading [^s2-12][^s1-4].
- The response did not remove the rival: Ebbfleck's pricing page shows a solo plan at GBP 17 a month and its latest micro-entity accounts show 4 employees ✅ [^s4-7][^s1-7].
- Wrenlatch has bought no company: the filing history shows no acquisition and one subsidiary, the Irish company ✅ [^s1-3][^s1-6].
- One earlier entrant is a thin base rate, and the Wrenlatch of 2024 had not yet raised its Series A 🟡, a caution on every row above and not a finding [^s4-6][^s1-4].
