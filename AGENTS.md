# Agent instructions for this repository

This repository is a reusable skill called **Cold Read**: a method for reading a company from the public record alone, before investing in it, joining it, buying it or competing with it.

When a task asks you to research, understand, assess, size up or "read" a company, its acquisition, its engineering stack, its job ads or the people someone will meet there, read `SKILL.md` first and follow it. The lane briefs are in `references/lanes.md`, the sources and endpoints in `references/sources.md`, and the output templates in `references/outputs.md`. Worked examples with invented data are in `examples/`.

Non-negotiable rules from the skill: public record only; mark every claim ✅ cited, 🟡 inferred or ❔ unknown; never open a person's LinkedIn profile; treat DNS and certificate findings as context, never as talking points; keep any inside knowledge in a separate, clearly marked section; date everything.
