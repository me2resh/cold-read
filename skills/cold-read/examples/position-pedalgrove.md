## The answer in one paragraph

Pedalgrove is second of four by fleet size and first by city coverage, and its moat is the repair network, not the bikes: the only intangible it has ever booked is "service partner agreements" ✅ [FY2024 accounts]. The leader, Velothane, is bigger on fleet and cheaper per month but is hiring for a repair network it does not yet have ✅ [12 of 30 open roles]. The ranking changes if Velothane's repair hiring lands or if the Transport for London grant scheme, which favours operators with in-house repair, is renewed in April 🟡.
